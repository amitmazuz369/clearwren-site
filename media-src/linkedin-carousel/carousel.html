How we measured, and what this does not show

A first measurement, with its limits.

  • 13 public Confluence Cloud sites. Found through ordinary web search, not randomly selected.
  • Up to 100 random pages per site. Margin of about 8 points on the headline figure.
  • Same engine as the product (32 rules today). Only certain failures count against a page.
  • 11 of 55 level A and AA criteria. The rest need a person, and no tool can certify accessibility.
  • Aggregate only. No site is named. We build a Confluence checker, so we have a stake.
