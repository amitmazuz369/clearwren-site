Support

Write to support@clearwren.com.

What to expect

  • First reply within one working day, Sunday to Thursday.
  • Bugs that block a scan or produce a wrong result are treated as urgent.
  • Support is in English or Hebrew.

What helps us answer quickly

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the space key involved
  • The page where you saw the problem, and what you expected instead
  • For a wrong finding: which check fired, and the content it fired on

Reporting a false positive

A check that fires on content which is genuinely fine is a bug in the check, and we want to hear about it. Send the content and the check name. Rules are versioned, and corrections ship to every site at once.

Restorable: asking about a deleted item

  • Your Atlassian site URL and the old key of the work item, for example ABC-123
  • Roughly when it was deleted, and whether it is on the Deleted work items page
  • If a restore failed, the message Jira or the app showed

We cannot see your Jira site or the copies the app keeps: they stay in your site. Documentation: Restorable docs.
