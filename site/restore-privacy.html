Privacy policy: Restorable for Jira

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page covers Restorable (listed as "Restorable: Restore Deleted Work Items for Jira"), our app for Jira Cloud. Our general privacy policy covers this website and our other apps.

Who we are

Clearwren builds apps for the Atlassian Marketplace. Contact: privacy@clearwren.com.

What the app stores

A deleted work item can only be recreated from a copy made before it was deleted. So while the app is installed, it keeps a copy of every work item in your site, and updates that copy each time the item is created, changed or commented on. Most copies therefore belong to items that are never deleted.

A copy holds the item's key, space, work type, summary, description, priority, labels, components, versions, due date, status, security level, the assignee and reporter account IDs, the values of all custom fields, links to other items, the names, sizes, upload times and uploaders of attached files (not the files themselves), and its comments with their authors and dates. Text is copied in full, including any people mentioned in it. When an item is deleted, the copy also records when, and the account ID of the person who deleted it, if Jira provides it.

The copies are written to Forge storage, which sits inside Atlassian's platform, in your Jira site's app storage, under your agreement with Atlassian. We do not operate servers that receive your data, we have no access to your Forge storage, and the app sends nothing outside Atlassian's platform.

Who can see it

Only Jira administrators can open the app's page. It lists deleted items only from spaces the viewer may browse, and it hides the summary of items that had a security level. A restore runs with the permissions of the person who presses Restore, and keeps the item's security level.

Personal data

The copies contain the account IDs and display names already in your Jira site (assignees, reporters, comment authors, uploaders, the person who deleted an item, people mentioned in text), and whatever personal data your work items themselves contain, for example in summaries, descriptions, comments or custom fields. The app collects nothing beyond what is in Jira.

Your organisation decides what goes into its Jira site and why. We process this data only to provide the app, and only inside Atlassian's platform.

About once a week the app reports the account IDs it holds to Atlassian's personal data reporting service, as Atlassian requires of Forge apps. When Atlassian reports that an account was closed, the app replaces that person's name with "Former user" in its copies and removes their account ID from the assignee, reporter, comment author, uploader and mention fields. It does not scan free text or custom fields: a name typed into a summary, description or comment, or a person chosen in a custom field, stays in the copy until the copy is removed.

Analytics and tracking

The app sends no telemetry and calls nothing outside Atlassian's platform. Its only calls are to your Jira site and to Atlassian's personal data reporting service.

Retention and deletion

  • A copy of a work item that still exists is kept while the app is installed, and updated when the item changes.
  • A copy of a deleted item is kept for 90 days, then removed by a nightly job.
  • A copy is removed as soon as its item is restored; the restored item gets a fresh copy.
  • When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's Forge storage under its own data lifecycle for Forge apps: the data is first marked as deleted, can be relinked if the app is reinstalled within 21 days, and is then removed permanently at the end of Atlassian's retention period. See Atlassian's data lifecycle for Forge-hosted storage.

To remove stored copies without uninstalling, contact support and we will provide the steps.

Support correspondence

Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to resolve the matter and for our records afterwards. Ask us to delete them and we will.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced on the app's Marketplace listing before they take effect.
