## Engagement Schedule

### Daily (10 min)
1. **Morning (9am):** Comment on 5 posts in #confluence hashtag
   - Find posts with 50-500 likes (sweet spot)
   - Leave 2-3 sentence genuine comment
   - Example: "Great point on X — we built Clearwren specifically for this use case"

2. **Afternoon (3pm):** Engage with @confluence, @atlassian posts
   - Like + comment on 3-5 recent posts
   - Follow 5 new accounts (filter: Confluence/accessibility interest)

3. **Evening (6pm):** Respond to any comments on @clearwren posts

### Weekly (30 min)

- **Friday 9am:** Post carousel #2
