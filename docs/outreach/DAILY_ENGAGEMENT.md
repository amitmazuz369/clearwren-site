# Daily Instagram Engagement — Week 1 Action Plan

**Goal:** Build followers from 2 → 50+ through organic engagement

---

## Daily Checklist (10 min/day)

### Morning (9am)
**Comment on 5 posts in #confluence hashtag**

1. Search: `#confluence` on Instagram
2. Filter by: Last 24 hours, 100-1000 likes (sweet spot)
3. Read the caption — write a genuine 2-3 line comment
4. Example comments:

```
"This approach to documentation structure is exactly what we're 
building for. Love seeing teams prioritize clarity for everyone."
```

```
"Great breakdown! Screen readers + users who skim both need this. 
The best teams document with accessibility-first thinking."
```

**Do NOT:**
- Copy-paste exact same comment twice
- Leave one-word comments ("Nice!")
- Spam unrelated hashtags

### Afternoon (3pm)
**Engage with @confluence, @atlassian posts (5 min)**

1. Visit @confluence profile
2. Comment on 2-3 recent posts (same approach as morning)
3. Follow 5 new accounts (search: Confluence + a11y keywords)

### Evening (6pm)
**Check @clearwren for replies (2 min)**

- Respond to any DMs immediately (personalized)
- Heart/reply to comments on your posts
- Note anyone asking questions

**Total daily time:** 17 minutes
