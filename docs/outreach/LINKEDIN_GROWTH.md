**Search Strategy:**

- `#confluence accessibility`

## Comment Do's & Don'ts

### ✅ DO:
- Reference data from our study ("1,179 pages", "57%", "83%")
- Be specific to their industry/role
- Ask a genuine follow-up question
- Keep it 2-3 sentences max
- Use their name if relevant

### ❌ DON'T:
- Sell directly ("try Clearwren!")
- Copy-paste same comment twice
- Write one-liner comments
- Leave generic praise ("Great post!")
- Link to your site in first comment

### Example Good Comment:
```
This resonates with what we're seeing. 
We audited 13 public Confluence instances and found accessibility gaps 
on 57% of them — mostly missing alt text and broken heading hierarchy. 
How are you prioritizing these fixes across your team?
```

### Example Bad Comment:
```
Love this! Check out Clearwren at clearwren.com for more insights.
```
